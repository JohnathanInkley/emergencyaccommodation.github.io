Dear Mayor Fiaz,

I am a member of the London Renters Union, a membership organisation of more than 7000 renters in the private sector and housing association properties that takes action to improve members’ housing situations through legal action, advocacy, media work and protest. 

I am writing to you today to ask you to meet urgently with the Newham Gypsy and Traveller community and ourselves in the next two weeks, by the 27th of April. 

As you know, Newham Council has been allocated £2m of funding to make a site for the local Gypsy and Traveller community. This is to make up for the forced displacement of families as the result of the 2012 Olympics. Yet so far the Council have taken no action at all and these families are in desperate need of a safe place to build a life.

I am aware that you were supposed to meet the families back in February, but cancelled that meeting. Given that the funding is time-bound and the clock is ticking, it is important you meet with the families as soon as possible. 

Yours sincerely,
